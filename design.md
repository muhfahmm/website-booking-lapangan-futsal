# Design System: Futsal Court Booking

## 1. Color Palette (Modern Turf Theme)
- **Primary (Field Green):** `bg-emerald-600` (#059669) - *Main action & brand identity.*
- **Secondary (Dark Pitch):** `bg-slate-900` (#0f172a) - *Sidebar, footer, & typography.*
- **Accent (Highlight):** `text-yellow-400` (#facc15) - *Call-to-action & pricing tag.*
- **Surface (Clean):** `bg-gray-50` (#f9fafb) - *Main body background.*
- **Neutral (Border/Subtle):** `border-gray-200` (#e5e7eb).

## 2. Typography
- **Headings:** Inter / Montserrat (Bold, tight tracking).
- **Body:** Inter / Roboto (Regular, high readability).

## 3. UI Components Style
- **Cards (Lapangan):** - Style: `bg-white`, `border-l-4 border-emerald-600`, `shadow-sm`, `hover:shadow-lg`.
- **Buttons:** - Primary: `rounded-lg`, `bg-emerald-600`, `text-white`, `transition-all`.
  - Secondary (Book Now): `rounded-lg`, `bg-yellow-400`, `text-slate-900`, `font-bold`.
- **Admin Sidebar:** - `bg-slate-900`, `text-emerald-50` with active state `bg-emerald-700`.

## 4. Visual Elements
- **Icons:** Lucide-React or Heroicons (Outline style).
- **Images:** High-quality photos of the field with a subtle dark overlay for better text contrast.
- **Spacing:** Generous padding/margin (Tailwind `p-6`, `gap-6`) for "airy" and professional look.
